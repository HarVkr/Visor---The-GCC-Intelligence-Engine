## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the calculated discretion and authoritative poise of a top-tier institutional strategy memo combined with the high-impact visual clarity of a board-level executive pitch deck. Crafted for institutional intelligence workflows, the aesthetic rejects sensory overload, superficial ornamentation, and chaotic dashboard densities in favor of calm, spacious, and decisive presentation surfaces.

### Emotional Signature & Demeanor
- **Sovereign & Grounded:** Establishes instantaneous credibility through disciplined proportions, editorial whitespace, and zero visual friction.
- **Analytical Precision:** Data points, executive summaries, and critical performance indicators are highlighted with meticulous typographic care and unambiguous contrast.
- **Understated Elegance:** Avoids consumer-grade playfulness, synthetic saturated gradients, and excessive motion; communicates power through architectural alignment, crisp rule lines, and restrained color deployment.

### Aesthetic Paradigm: Architectural Executive Minimal
The visual identity draws from high-end corporate presentation decks and private equity prospectuses:
- Crisp, luminous off-white canvas grounding the interface.
- Low-contrast structural grids using hairline slate borders that frame rather than trap information.
- Monolithic numeric readouts juxtaposed against warm, readable institutional body copy.

## Colors

The palette simulates the ink, paper, and refined focal inks of bespoke board decks. Color acts strictly as an analytical tool—directing focus, signaling performance, and denoting high-conviction actions.

### Canvas & Surface Hierarchy
- **Base Canvas (`#F8FAFC`):** An airy, light slate tinted white. Reduces retina fatigue while presenting a stark, crisp field for black and slate typography.
- **Card / Deck Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Pristine white cards floating with hairline delineation over the canvas to anchor discrete institutional modules.
- **Muted Inset Surface (`#F1F5F9`):** Used sparingly for table headers, embedded metric sub-panels, and secondary callouts.

### Functional Inks
- **Primary Ink — Deep Institutional Slate (`#0F172A`):** The primary color anchor. Used for primary typography, dominant headers, high-conviction active states, and primary executive action triggers.
- **Secondary Ink — Refined Cobalt (`#2563EB`):** Applied exclusively to active selections, key interactive trajectories, navigation signals, and primary trend lines.
- **Tertiary Ink — Executive Sage Emerald (`#059669`):** A sophisticated deep emerald denoting positive return on investment, upward institutional alpha, and healthy operational health.
- **Warning & Delta Neutralizer (`#D97706`):** Deep amber used for strategic variances or pending actions requiring board review.
- **Critical Variance (`#DC2626`):** Subdued crimson reserved for systemic risks, downside deltas, and compliance alerts.

### Structural Dividers & Neutral Shades
- **Rule Lines & Outlines (`#E2E8F0`):** Hairline precision borders providing structure without visual density.
- **Secondary Ink / Metadata (`#475569`):** Supporting analytical descriptions and axis definitions.
- **Tertiary Ink / Inactive (`#94A3B8`):** Column labels, timestamps, and resting state indicators.

## Typography

The typographical pairing provides clear structural hierarchy: **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides modern, confident executive authority for titles and data metrics, while **Inter** delivers neutral, hyper-legible utility for analytical prose and dense data layouts.

### Tabular Numerical Treatment
- All numbers, performance metrics, and financial tables must enforce tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums; font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1;`). This maintains vertical alignment across rows and rapid executive scanning across multi-column data views.

### Typographic Roles
- **Eyebrow Categories (`label-eyebrow`):** Rendered in uppercase with generous tracking to establish immediate analytical categorization (e.g., `INSTITUTIONAL ALLOCATION`, `ANNUALIZED ALPHA`).
- **Data KPIs (`kpi-metric-lg`, `kpi-metric-md`):** Scaled dramatically against card content, allowing board members to absorb core metrics at a glance.
- **Narrative Blocks (`body-lead`, `body-default`):** Kept to an optimal reading width of 60 to 72 characters with comfortable line heights to reflect the editorial pacing of white papers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is architected around a presentation-slide ethos: bounded, intentional visual segments arranged within a fluid 12-column grid. Modules do not crowd one another; wide margins and gutters create an unhurried, executive-level atmosphere.

### Grid & Composition Rules
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12-column fluid grid, maximum content width constrained to `1440px` for presentation legibility. Gutters are locked at `gutter-lg` (`2rem`), outer margins at `margin-lg` (`3rem`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout. Gutters shift to `gutter` (`1.5rem`), outer margins to `margin` (`2rem`). Multicolumn decks collapse into 2-up or stacked presentation cards.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout. Gutters compress to `gutter-sm` (`1rem`), margins to `margin-sm` (`1rem`). Full horizontal expansion for financial tables with horizontal scroll indicators.

### Spacing Cadence
- Content components adhere strictly to the 4px baseline grid.
- **Slide & Section Anchors:** High-level executive thematic shifts rely on `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) gaps between module groups.
- **Card Interiors:** Standard card padding is strictly uniform at `space-lg` (`1.5rem`), scaling down to `space-md` (`1rem`) on small viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy shadows, saturated glows, and complex skeuomorphic extrusions. Depth is established through structural layering and soft, daylight ambient shadowing.

### Structural Layering
- **Surface Level 0 (Canvas):** `#F8FAFC`. The foundational backdrop.
- **Surface Level 1 (Presentation Cards):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an exact 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and an ambient, low-contrast shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02);`
- **Surface Level 2 (Elevated Strategy Trays & Context Flyouts):** `#FFFFFF` with refined slate definition:
  - `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02);`
  - Border: 1px solid `#CBD5E1`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Board Modals & Intelligence Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` centered over a neutral backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(4px); background-color: rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)`):
  - `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`

### Hairline Rules & Dividers
Dividers must remain discreet. Never use pure black or high-contrast grey lines. Use `#E2E8F0` at 1px thickness without shadow for card headers, table row dividers, and modular separations.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a tailored, architectural radius profile (`roundedness: 1`), conveying corporate discipline and crisp drafting precision over casual consumer softness.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Core Elements (`rounded` / 0.25rem / 4px):** Form controls, inputs, dropdown menus, inline table badges, and buttons. Maintains geometric precision.
- **Presentation Containers (`rounded-lg` / 0.5rem / 8px):** Primary slide decks, intelligence cards, table envelopes, and executive overview panels. Softens layout transitions without rounding off the sharp corporate character.
- **Dialogs & Flyouts (`rounded-xl` / 0.75rem / 12px):** Popover overlays and modal dialogs.
- **Pill Elements (Full Radius):** Restricted strictly to circular status dots and executive metric indicator tags (e.g., `+14.8% ROI`). Never used for structural cards or actionable buttons.

## Components

### Buttons & Executive CTAs
- **Primary Button:** `#0F172A` background with crisp `#FFFFFF` text. Minimal hover transition shifting to `#1E293B`. Border radius `4px`. Height `40px` (desktop), padding `0 1.25rem`. Typography: `font-size: 0.875rem; font-weight: 600`.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background, 1px solid border `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`. Hover: Background `#F1F5F9`.
- **Cobalt Accent Action (Direct Drill-down):** `#2563EB` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Hover: `#1D4ED8`. Used exclusively for forward-looking analytical transitions.

### Cards & Deck Slides
- **Background:** `#FFFFFF`.
- **Border:** 1px solid `#E2E8F0`.
- **Radius:** `8px` (`rounded-lg`).
- **Internal Spacing:** `1.5rem` (`space-lg`).
- **Header Structure:** Must include an uppercase tracking eyebrow (`label-eyebrow`) in `#64748B`, followed immediately by a card title (`title-card`) in `#0F172A`.

### Data Chips & Status Badges
- **Positive Delta / ROI:** `#ECFDF5` background, `#059669` text, 1px solid `#A7F3D0` border. Radius `9999px`. Font: `0.75rem`, `font-weight: 600`, tabular numerals.
- **Neutral / Informational:** `#F1F5F9` background, `#475569` text, 1px solid `#E2E8F0`.
- **Variance Attention:** `#FEF3C7` background, `#D97706` text, 1px solid `#FDE68A`.

### Tables & Intelligence Matrices
- **Table Container:** Wrapped in an isolated card frame with rounded corners.
- **Header Row:** Background `#F8FAFC`, bottom border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`. Cell text: `0.6875rem`, uppercase, `#64748B`, weight `600`, letter spacing `0.05em`.
- **Data Rows:** Height `48px`, alternating row striping avoided in favor of clean 1px hairline dividers (`#E2E8F0`). Cell typography: `0.875rem`, color `#0F172A`. Hover state: `#F8FAFC`.
- **Numerical Alignment:** Quantitative figures right-aligned with `tnum` enabled.

### Form Inputs & Select Controls
- **Default State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, radius `4px`, height `38px`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`.
- **Focus State:** 1px solid `#2563EB` with subtle outer glow ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`).

### Executive Presentation Specifics
- **Executive Stat Block:** Vertical layout with `label-eyebrow` on top, followed by `kpi-metric-lg` in `#0F172A`, followed by an inline baseline shift containing the delta badge and comparison timeline text in `caption-meta`.
- **Section Dividers:** Spaced at `2.5rem`, featuring a hairline rule paired with a left-anchored numeral or section indicator for slide-like navigation.
